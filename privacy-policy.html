Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Data We Process

Autoo Bot runs on your local machine. We do not operate a central server that collects your personal data. API keys and TikTok tokens are stored only on your device in the configuration folder you choose.

2. TikTok Data

When you connect your TikTok account via OAuth, TikTok provides an access token. This token is stored locally and used only to post videos on your behalf. We do not receive, store, or transmit your TikTok login credentials.

3. Third Parties

Autoo Bot communicates with: TikTok (Content Posting API), and optionally Kling AI and Google (for video generation and translation). Each service has its own privacy policy. We do not sell or share your data.

4. Contact

For privacy questions: use the contact method provided in the app configuration.
